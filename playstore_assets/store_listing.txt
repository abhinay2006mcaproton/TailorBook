TailorBook: Boutique Manager

2. SHORT DESCRIPTION (Max 80 characters):
Digital ledger, measurements, orders, and UPI receipts for tailoring boutiques.

3. FULL DESCRIPTION (Max 4000 characters):
TailorBook is an offline-first smart digital ledger and management app designed specifically for boutique owners, fashion designers, and master tailors. Replace traditional paper measurement books and scattered diaries with a fast, organized, and secure digital notebook that works 100% offline.

KEY FEATURES FOR YOUR BOUTIQUE:

✂️ CUSTOM GARMENT MEASUREMENTS
• Pre-built measurement templates for Blouse, Kurti, Salwar, Churidars, Gown, Shirts, and Trousers.
• Move key points like Shoulder and Length to the top for rapid recording.
• Immutable measurement version history (v1, v2, v3) so you always know what changed.
• Add custom measurement points and standard stitching prices per garment.

📋 ORDERS & DELIVERY SCHEDULE
• Multi-garment order booking in a single customer invoice (e.g., 2 blouses + 1 lehenga).
• Quick delivery presets (3 days, 5 days, 1 week, 2 weeks) with automated delivery calendar.
• Assign cutting masters and tracking order statuses from New to Cutting, Stitching, and Ready.
• Record stitching instruction voice notes and attach neck, sleeve, and fabric reference photos.

💳 DYNAMIC UPI QR & ADVANCE COLLECTION
• Present an instant on-screen UPI QR Code (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM) to collect advances on the spot.
• Automatic calculation of total bill, advance paid, and pending balance.
• Direct peer-to-peer customer payment to your shop's bank account with 0% commission.

🔒 100% OFFLINE-FIRST & PRIVATE
• Full database stored locally on your device — works seamlessly in basements and areas with zero internet.
• One-tap database backup export to your phone, Google Drive, or WhatsApp.
• Zero personal data shared or sold to third parties.

🌐 MULTI-LANGUAGE SUPPORT
• Full interface support in English and Telugu (తెలుగు).
• Indian currency numbering format (₹500, ₹1,500, ₹25,000).

Whether you run a solo tailoring shop or a multi-master designer boutique, TailorBook saves you hours of paperwork, eliminates measurement disputes, and keeps your orders delivered on time.

Download TailorBook today and modernize your boutique!
